01 / Why we study

Social interaction is a multilevel psychological process.

We study the cognitive, emotional, interpersonal, and contextual factors associated with social difficulties and their consequences for mental health.

✦

Empirical questions, transparent methods.

Evidence guides both explanation and intervention.

Research themes

Research topics
and questions.

Our projects examine social interaction, mental health, and intervention across psychological, interpersonal, developmental, and cultural levels.

Social Anxiety

How we work

Methods for studying
social interaction.

We combine complementary methods to test mechanisms and evaluate interventions across experience, behavior, physiology, and context.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Qualitative inquiry and lived experience

  2. 02

    Measure

    Surveys, psychometrics, and longitudinal designs

  3. 03

    Observe

    Behavioral experiments and eye tracking

  4. 04

    Map

    Psychophysiology and neuroimaging

  5. 05

    Model

    AI-assisted, interpretable analysis of social interaction

  6. 06

    Translate

    Precise, practical, evidence-based interventions

Principal investigator

Muyu Lin, PhD

Research Professor · Doctoral Supervisor · Clinical Psychologist

Dr. Lin studies the mechanisms and intervention pathways of social difficulties using multimodal methods. Her work spans social anxiety, autism, interpersonal trauma, positive psychology, cross-cultural psychology, and implementation science.
